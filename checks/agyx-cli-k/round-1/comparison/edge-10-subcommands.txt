# Edge Fixture: 10 Subcommands Matrix

1. `text`: Line-by-line comparison with -i, -b, -u, -w.
2. `folder`: Hierarchical tree comparison with -i, -d, -r, --content, --include, --exclude.
3. `hex`: Byte-by-byte alignment comparison with --max-hunks.
4. `merge`: 3-way ancestor/ours/theirs merge with --output and conflict markers.
5. `table`: Delimited CSV/TSV table comparison with --delimiter and --no-header.
6. `sync`: Two-way folder synchronization planning and --apply execution.
7. `picture`: Pixel-by-pixel PNG comparison with --heatmap output.
8. `folder-merge`: 3-way folder tree merge planning and --apply execution.
9. `audio`: Audio waveform/sample comparison.
10. `session`: Saved session document loading and tab execution.
